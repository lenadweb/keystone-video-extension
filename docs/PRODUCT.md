# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Projector owners watching browser videos, often in a dim room. They need to square the picture without interrupting playback or navigating the projector's menus.

## Product purpose

Apply vertical and horizontal perspective correction to HTML video. Keep settings local and adjustment quick. The existing popup is the approved interface; publication work must preserve its layout, controls, typography and colors.

## Brand personality

Calm, precise, compact. The publication brief calls for an original logo inspired by Android 15 and Material Design 3. Extend the existing blue identity and Manrope typography into the external promotional materials.

## Anti-references

No mascot, glossy badge, decorative 3D or imitation Google/Android identity. Do not imply endorsement, store availability or compatibility with every protected player.

## Design principles

- Demonstrate the actual extension rather than a redesigned mockup.
- Make the projection metaphor recognizable at toolbar size.
- Keep claims specific to implemented behavior.
- Preserve the approved application UI.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Retain the popup's labeled controls, keyboard operation and native range inputs. Promotional headlines must remain readable at half size. The logo must work on light and dark backgrounds and in one color.
